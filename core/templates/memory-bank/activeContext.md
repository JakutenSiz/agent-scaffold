# Active Context

*Belongs here: what is actively being worked on right now — recent changes, open decisions,
current risks, temporary constraints, next steps. Does NOT belong here: stable architecture (see
`systemPatterns.md`) or a full history of everything ever done (see `progress.md`, and the archive
once entries age out).*

> **RULE.** Newest entry first, dated `## YYYY-MM-DD <title>`. Entries older than
> `memoryBank.windowDays` days (default 14) with no pending marker move to `memory-bank/archive/`
> (`node scripts/memory-bank-archive.mjs <repo> --write`); an entry with a pending marker stays regardless of age.
